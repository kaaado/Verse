# Task 04 — Risks & Open Decisions

## 1. Task ID & Title
`task-04-risks-and-open-decisions` — Risk Analysis, Confidentiality Boundaries & Open Decisions

## 2. Objective
Identify technical, confidentiality, performance, and legal risks, and catalog open decisions requiring Product Owner resolution.

## 3. Scope
- Threat and risk matrix (confidentiality, free-tier limits, WebGL performance).
- Client non-disclosure boundaries (hospitals, commercial startups).
- Catalog open decisions for owner review.

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
- Risk matrix with mitigations documented.
- Open decisions list formulated for owner sign-off.
